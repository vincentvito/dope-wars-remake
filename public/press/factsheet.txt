Play Dope Wars is an independent browser remake inspired by the Drug Wars / Dope Wars trading-game formula. Start with $2,000 cash and $5,000 debt, compare prices across six New York districts, and manage risk through a 30-day Classic run. Pro adds labs, assets, weapons and international destinations unlocked during play.
